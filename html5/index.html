A QUICK WARM-UP
Meet you at the tree.

    
You’re Snoopy. Move between five branches to meet the incoming balls, then time your swing as they reach your racket. Each return earns 2 points.

    
Lucy sometimes sends a ball back faster. Return those for 3 points. Miss three balls and the game is over. The serves get busier the longer you play.
