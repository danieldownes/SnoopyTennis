Snoopy Tennis v2.1


Keys

 Up - Up arrow OR 'P'
 Down - Down arrow OR 'L'
 Hit - Space bar
 End Current Game - 'Q'
 


Objective

You are snoopy. You move up and down the tree so to hit any balls advancing
towards you. If any ball should pass you, you lose 1 of 3 lives.
The aim is to hit ALL the balls, no matter how intense it gets! The balls are
served at random time intervals and at random heights. The time intervals get
shorter as the game progresses.
Some times Lucy re-deflects your balls at a faster speed, you get extra points
when hitting these.
